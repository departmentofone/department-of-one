02 The live one

The app I run myself.

FitLog

Live

A workout log, a meal and macro tracker and a fasting timer in one app. I built it for my own training, because the trackers I tried felt bloated and I wanted all three in one place. Everything I would do for your app, I did here first: the design, the code, the store listing and the support inbox.

Also in it
Personal records, trend charts, and workout and meal programs you can reuse
Offline
Log a workout with no signal. It syncs when you're back online.
Install
Runs in the browser, and you can add it to your home screen without going through an app store.
