04 About

Who's behind it.

Just me. I build apps, sites and bots, and I like finishing what I start.

Away from the keyboard I read a lot: Tolkien and Sanderson for fiction, and anything about finance, a world I find fascinating.

  • I use what I make

    FitLog started as something I wanted for myself, so I am usually the first to notice when it breaks.

  • You'll reach me

    There is no account manager to get past. The person reading your message is the one who will write the code.

  • No tricks

    No fake countdowns and no pressure. If something costs money, it will say so plainly.

  • Your data stays yours

    Nothing gets sold, to advertisers or to anyone else, and you can delete your FitLog account whenever you like.

Find me

Running FitLog still costs something, between hosting, the database and my time. If it has earned a spot on your phone, you can buy me a coffee. It's optional and doesn't unlock anything.
