Hi, I'm Milan. I make small things.

Department of One is just me, messing around and building whatever I think people might enjoy. A fitness app, an endless ink painting, a timer with a plant in it.

Everything on the shelf is free and open source. I build the things I want to exist, finish them, and put them here. Some will be useful, some will be pointless in a fun way, and that's fine.

Away from the keyboard I read a lot: Tolkien and Sanderson for fiction, and anything about finance, a world I find fascinating.

Found a bug, or want to say hi? Write to departmentofone.app@gmail.com. I read everything.

Keep the lights on
