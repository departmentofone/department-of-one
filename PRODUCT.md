# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
People who find the studio through FitLog, a shared link or GitHub, deciding within seconds whether this developer is worth trusting and whether to open the app. A second audience is people paying for an app they think should be free, who might send a request.

## Product Purpose
Department of One is the portfolio of one developer who builds free apps alone, with no ads, no premium tier and no investors. The homepage exists to get visitors to open FitLog (the one live app) and to leave the developer's reputation higher than they found it. Success: visitors open FitLog; some send app requests.

## Positioning
Every job, from code to the support inbox, is done by the same person, and everything shipped is free for everyone. The person reading a bug report is the one who wrote the bug.

## Operating Context
Static HTML/CSS/JS on Vercel (department-of-one.vercel.app). Copy on index.html is live-editable: elements with `data-content-key` are overwritten from the Supabase `site_content` table (content.js), edited through admin.html. contact.html posts to /api. admin.html reuses the site tokens and `.btn`, `.wrap`, `.wordmark`.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- FitLog: workout log, meal and macro tracker, fasting timer, PRs, trend charts, reusable programs, offline logging with sync, installable PWA, full export and account deletion. URL: https://fitlog-two-gamma.vercel.app
- A second app is in progress; nothing public about it yet.
- Three-state theme (system/light/dark) exists; keeping it is optional.

## Brand Commitments
- Name "Department of One" is a solo-dev joke, not a real department; no bureaucracy props.
- The logo is the lowercase mono wordmark "department of one" with a blinking block cursor. Must be kept, and the cursor must visibly blink.
- The Requests section (asking for free versions of paid apps) must be kept.
- Owner's stated feel: a pristine, well-kept gym or a luxury resort lobby; premium, elegant, smooth motion; never flat.
- Human-facing copy passes the human-writing rules: no em dashes, no AI tells, no sales language.

## Evidence on Hand
- FitLog screenshots, 1080x1920: FitLog/store-listing/phone-screenshots/1-workouts, 2-set-logging, 3-meals, 4-community, 5-diets, 6-more (captured 2026-09-24).
- FitLog icon: assets/fitlog-icon.png.
- No testimonials, user counts, press or reviews exist. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles
- Free means every feature for everyone; never imply a paid tier.
- Show the work instead of claiming quality.
- One person's voice, plain and a little dry.
