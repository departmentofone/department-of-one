# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
People who like finding small, free, slightly odd things on the internet: useful tools, toys, wallpapers and apps. They arrive from a shared link, GitHub or one of the apps, and browse the shelf.

## Product Purpose
Department of One is one person making things for fun and giving them away. The homepage is a small app-shop-style shelf of free, open source projects. Success: people open the live apps, come back when new ones land, and some buy a coffee.

## Operating Context
Static HTML/CSS/JS on Vercel (www.departmentofone.net). Pages are generated by `tools/shelf.py` (run `python tools/shelf.py`); edit the SHELF list there to add a project, change its status or give it a link. A few elements carry `data-content-key` and are overwritten from Supabase `site_content` (content.js), edited in admin.html. /api/contact remains only for FitLog's in-app feedback form. The services offering (inquiry form, /pay quote pages) was removed on 2026-10-23; old URLs redirect.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Live: FitLog (https://fitlog-two-gamma.vercel.app).
- Shan Shui (procedural ink-landscape wallpaper) exists on the owner's other PC; files to come.
- Everything else on the shelf is a placeholder idea marked "Soon".
- Everything is free and open source. No prices, no paid tiers promised either way.

## Brand Commitments
- Name stays "Department of One". New mark: "Mr. One", a 1 with blinking eyes on a tomato tile. The old mono wordmark with the blinking cursor is kept on /brand in case the owner prefers it.
- Buy me a coffee is prominent: header button, hero button, tip jar section, About and footer.
- The idea box (id `requests`) stays.
- Human-facing copy passes the human-writing rules.
