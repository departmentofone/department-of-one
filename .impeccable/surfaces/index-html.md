# Surface brief: homepage (index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: visitors deciding whether to trust one developer and open FitLog; secondary, people who want a free version of a paid app.
Action: Open FitLog (primary); send a request (secondary, section must stay).
Constraints: keep the mono typing wordmark with a visibly blinking brass cursor in every motion setting; keep Requests; keep data-content-key hooks; copy may be rewritten under the human-writing rules; no invented proof.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The studio as a small hotel's front desk, kept by one person: a numbered brass key for every app, a concierge who takes requests. Refuses the flat dark portfolio hero with a phone mockup block.

OWN-WORLD: Two lights on one set of materials. Night (dark): verde marble green-black, pools of warm lamp light, brushed brass. Day (light): honed travertine, cool daylight, brass darkened to bronze for contrast. Bodoni Moda display, Hanken Grotesk body, JetBrains Mono only for the wordmark. Brass hairlines, engraved numerals, oval key fobs; nothing is flat, every surface has stone grain and light falloff.
Raise, from the drawcord cape: brass is the single accent and it always means "you can act here"; nothing decorative is brass except the cursor.
Raise, from the night six-pack: all motion is damped and weighted, like a heavy door on a closer; nothing snaps.
Raise, from the cloud edge: colour lives at edges and light, text fields stay achromatic.

STORY: Visitor sees one person runs this, meets FitLog as key No. 1 through a slow slideshow of real screens, reads the house rules, opens FitLog or leaves a request at the desk.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, a huge Bodoni headline over a lamp-lit marble field; right, the FitLog slideshow in a slim brass-edged frame at room scale, crossfading real screens with captions; "Open FitLog" brass button under the lede. Wordmark top left.

FORM: grounded candidate 3 of 7 (the front desk and its key board), seed 0a92ea42.
