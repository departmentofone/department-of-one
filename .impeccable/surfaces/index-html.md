# Surface brief: homepage (index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: visitors deciding whether to trust one developer and open FitLog; secondary, people who want a free version of a paid app.
Action: Open FitLog (primary); send a request (secondary, section must stay).
Constraints: keep the mono typing wordmark with a visibly blinking brass cursor in every motion setting; keep Requests; keep data-content-key hooks; copy may be rewritten under the human-writing rules; no invented proof. Owner's machine: Windows Chrome, 125% scaling, reduced motion on; his screenshot is the acceptance test.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The studio as a small hotel's front desk, kept by one person: a numbered brass key for every app, a concierge who takes requests. Refuses the flat portfolio hero with a phone mockup block.

OWN-WORLD: Two rooms on one set of materials. Night: verde marble, warm lamp pools, brushed brass (a real brushed raster, never a flat fill). Day (rebuilt 2026-09-27, owner chose the warm daylight lobby): cream Crema-Marfil-style marble with honey veins at its natural range, afternoon sun thrown through a tall window as a soft shadow plate across the stone, deep verde leather with brass trim for the primary action, warm ink. Day depth comes from shadow, never from white glow. Bodoni Moda display (a lower optical size and heavier weight by day so hairlines hold on Windows), Hanken Grotesk body and captions, JetBrains Mono only for the wordmark.
Raise, from the drawcord cape: brass marks what you can act on; hairline brass is structure.
Raise, from the night six-pack: motion is damped and weighted; nothing snaps.
Raise, from the cloud edge: colour lives at edges and light, text fields stay achromatic.

STORY: Visitor sees one person runs this, handles FitLog's real screens in a carousel they can drag, reads the house rules, opens FitLog or leaves a request at the desk.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, the Bodoni headline, lede and the Open FitLog action. Right, the signature interaction: a three-deep carousel of real FitLog screens (night screens by night, light screens by day). One screen at the front, its neighbours set back to either side, smaller and dimmer, so the stack reads as something to handle. Drag with finger or mouse, scroll the wheel over it, click a neighbour, use the dots or the arrow keys. A wide, low glow behind the whole stack, no brass outline around the screens. Caption in Hanken beneath.

FORM: grounded candidate 3 of 7 (the front desk and its key board), seed 0a92ea42.
