# Surface brief: homepage (index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: visitors deciding whether to trust one developer and open FitLog; secondary, people with an idea for an app.
Action: Open FitLog (primary); send an app idea (secondary, section must stay).
Constraints: keep the mono typing wordmark with a visibly blinking jade cursor and the "// ... for now" comment line under it in every motion setting; keep the Ideas section (id `requests`); no pricing promises (no free-forever or no-premium claims); keep data-content-key hooks; copy may be rewritten under the human-writing rules; no invented proof. Owner's machine: Windows Chrome, 125% scaling, reduced motion on; his screenshot is the acceptance test.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The studio as a small hotel's front desk, kept by one person: a numbered jade key tag for every app, a concierge who takes requests. Refuses the flat portfolio hero with a phone mockup block.

OWN-WORLD: Two rooms on one set of materials. Night: verde marble, warm lamp pools, lit jade for anything you can press (a lit gradient, never a flat fill), steel hardware. Day (rebuilt 2026-09-27, owner chose the warm daylight lobby): cream Crema-Marfil-style marble with honey veins at its natural range, afternoon sun thrown through a tall window as a soft shadow plate across the stone, deep verde leather with a jade edge for the primary action, warm ink. Day depth comes from shadow, never from white glow. Geist for display, body and captions; Geist Mono (the logo face) for the wordmark, its comment line and naming numbers.
Raise, from the drawcord cape: jade marks what you can act on; steel is hardware. (Brass and Bodoni were retired 2026-09-27: gold read as off-key, and Bodoni hairlines broke up on phones. Geist + Geist Mono now, matching the logo.)
Raise, from the night six-pack: motion is damped and weighted; nothing snaps.
Raise, from the cloud edge: colour lives at edges and light, text fields stay achromatic.

STORY: Visitor sees one person runs this, handles FitLog's real screens in a carousel they can drag, reads the house rules, opens FitLog or leaves a request at the desk.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, the Geist headline (its "one" in Geist Mono, jade), lede and the Open FitLog action. Right, the signature interaction: a three-deep carousel of real FitLog screens (night screens by night, light screens by day). One screen at the front, its neighbours set back to either side, smaller and dimmer, so the stack reads as something to handle. Drag with finger or mouse, scroll the wheel over it, click a neighbour, use the dots or the arrow keys. A wide, low glow behind the whole stack, no brass outline around the screens. Caption in Hanken beneath.

FORM: grounded candidate 3 of 7 (the front desk and its key board), seed 0a92ea42.
