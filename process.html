03 Process

What the process looks like.

The same four steps for a one-page site or a full app. Revisions are included in every package.

  1. 01

    Send a brief

    Tell me what it should do and who it is for, plus two or three sites or apps you like. I reply with what fits and a rough scope.

  2. 02

    See a first version

    A private preview link, or a build you can tap through on your phone. The look is settled before the rest is built.

  3. 03

    Build the rest

    You review as it comes together and we adjust. Tests and speed checks run before anything goes to you.

  4. 04

    Launch

    It goes live on your hosting and in your accounts, with the source code and a plain-English handover guide.

About AI

I use AI tools to speed up coding and writing. I review and test every page, screen and command myself. If you want none of it, say so before we start.

Access

I never ask for passwords. Hosting, stores and bot accounts are shared through invites, and you can remove me when we are done.

Questions people ask

Do I own what you build?

Yes. It runs on your hosting and accounts, and you get the source code and the logins.

What will it cost?

It depends on the scope. Describe the project in the form below and I will reply with a price that fits it, before you commit to anything.

Which platform will you use for my website?

Whichever suits you. A hand-coded site is the fastest and costs nothing to host on Vercel or Netlify. WordPress, Shopify, Webflow and Framer are easier to edit yourself. I will recommend one after reading your brief.

Do I need store accounts for an app?

Only to publish: Apple's developer program is $99 a year and Google Play is $25 once, both in your name. I walk you through both. New personal Google Play accounts also have to run a closed test with 12 testers for 14 days before an app can go public.

Where does a bot run?

On hosting you own, such as a small VPS or a container host like Railway or Render. That is usually a few dollars a month. Bots never need your Discord password: the token goes straight into your hosting settings.

Can you fix or extend something that already exists?

Usually. I work on bots written in JavaScript, TypeScript or Python, apps in Flutter, React Native or Expo, and sites on the platforms above. Send me what it does and what you need.
