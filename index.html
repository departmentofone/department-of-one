01 Services

What I build.

Three kinds of project, one person on every one of them. Delivery times are typical for the size of the package. The price depends on the scope, so describe yours through the inquiry form.

01 / Websites

Websites, from one page to a full store.

A custom design for your business, coded by hand or built on WordPress, Shopify, Webflow or Framer when you want to edit it yourself. Checked on phone, tablet and desktop, and speed-tested before it ships.

  • Booking, payments, newsletter signup, a blog or an online store
  • Page titles, descriptions, sitemap and social share images
  • Set up on your own hosting and domain
