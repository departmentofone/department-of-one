What free means here.

  1. i

    Every feature, for everyone

    There's no premium tier and nothing to subscribe to. When a feature ships, everybody gets it.

  2. ii

    No ads, no ad trackers

    You won't see upsell pop-ups either, since there's nothing to upsell.

  3. iii

    Your data stays yours

    Nothing gets sold, to advertisers or to anyone else. You can delete your account whenever you like.

  4. iv

    You'll reach me

    There's no support team to get past. The person reading your bug report is the one who wrote the bug.

Why it's free.

I've always admired the people behind The Pirate Bay, less for the controversy than for the idea: a few people built something useful and gave it to everyone for free.

For a long time I didn't know how I'd give something back in the same spirit. Building apps turned out to be my way. I make the ones I'd want to use and give them away, and since there are no investors, the only people I answer to are the ones using them.

Paying for an app you think should be free?

Tell me which one and what you use it for. If it's something I can build, I might make a free version of it. Call it the Robin Hood approach, minus the tights.
