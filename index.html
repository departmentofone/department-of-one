02Principles

A few rules I don't break.

  1. i.

    Free, for good

    No premium tier, no paywall, no features held back for a subscription. If it ships, everyone gets it.

  2. ii.

    No dark patterns

    No nagging upsells, no fake countdowns, nothing designed to trick you into anything.

  3. iii.

    Your data stays yours

    No ad trackers, and nothing gets sold. Delete your account whenever you like.

  4. iv.

    One person, fully accountable

    I write, test and maintain every line myself. When something breaks, you're talking to the person who fixes it.

03About

Why this exists

I've always admired the people behind The Pirate Bay. Not for the controversy, but for the idea underneath it: a handful of people can build something genuinely useful and hand it to everyone for free, with nobody at the door asking for money.

For a long time I didn't know how I'd give back in a similar way. Building apps turned out to be the answer. Tools anyone can use, free, for good - the way I always wished more software worked.
